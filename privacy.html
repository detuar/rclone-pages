Privacy Policy

Application: Rclone Google Drive Bridge (rclone-pages) • Effective Date: November 4, 2026 • Data Collected: None (0 Bytes)
Plain-English Summary: We Do Not Collect Any Data

Rclone Google Drive Bridge does not collect, store, log, proxy, transmit, or share any of your personal information, Google account details, OAuth tokens, or Google Drive files. All communication occurs strictly and directly between the rclone tool running on your own machine and Google's official API servers (googleapis.com).

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Rclone Google Drive Bridge (“the Application”, “we”, “our”) handles user data when you connect the open-source command-line utility rclone to your Google Drive account using our Google Cloud OAuth 2.0 Client credentials.

2. Information We Collect (Zero Collection)

We operate no backend servers, databases, analytics services, or telemetry collectors. Specifically:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect or see your name, email address, IP address, or Google Account profile information.
  • No Google Drive Content or Metadata: We never receive, view, cache, index, or store any of your Google Drive files, folder structures, or metadata.
  • No OAuth Credentials on External Servers: Your OAuth 2.0 authorization codes, access tokens, and refresh tokens are issued by Google directly to the loopback listener (127.0.0.1) or manual authorization flow on your local computer.
  • No Cookies or Tracking: This website is a static informational page hosted on GitHub Pages and sets no tracking cookies, fingerprinting scripts, or third-party advertising pixels.

3. How Google User Data Is Accessed and Used Locally

When you authorize the Application with your Google Account, you grant permission for your local installation of rclone to interact with the Google Drive API v3 on your behalf. Depending on the scope you select in rclone config (such as https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive, drive.file, or drive.readonly):

  • Local Execution Only: Data is accessed exclusively when you run rclone commands on your own device (e.g., listing directories, syncing folders, uploading backups, checking file hashes, or mounting your Drive).
  • Direct Point-to-Point Transfer: All file contents and API requests travel directly between your device and Google's servers over encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.2/1.3) connections.

4. Data Storage & Security

Because the Application has no remote infrastructure, all state resides exclusively on the device where you run rclone:

  • Local Configuration File: OAuth 2.0 tokens granted by Google are saved locally inside your personal rclone.conf file on your machine.
  • Encryption Options: You retain full control over securing your local configuration and data, including using rclone's built-in configuration file password encryption and rclone crypt client-side file encryption.

5. Data Sharing, Transfer & Third Parties

Because we never receive or possess your data, we never sell, rent, trade, transfer, or disclose any Google user data to any third party. Your Google user data is never used for advertising, profiling, market research, or training artificial intelligence / machine learning models.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use Disclosure)

Mandatory Limited Use Compliance Statement

Rclone Google Drive Bridge's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Data Retention, Deletion & Revoking Access

We retain zero user data. You have complete, immediate control over the OAuth tokens stored on your device and your Google Account authorization at any time:

  • Revoke via Google Account: Visit your Google Account Third-Party Connections & Permissions page and remove access for this application to immediately invalidate all issued tokens.
  • Delete Local Tokens: Run rclone config delete <remote-name> on your machine or delete the corresponding section in your local rclone.conf file.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, any changes will be committed publicly to the GitHub repository and published on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the OAuth configuration, please open an issue on the project repository at https://github.com/detuar/rclone-pages/issues.
